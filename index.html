Bruin Dine

Bruin Dine is a student-run organization founded in 2018 that works to bridge the gap between food waste and food insecurity by recovering food from UCLA dining halls that would have otherwise been thrown away and redistributing it to students and staff in need.

Follow on Instagram Learn More

How It Works

Bruin Dine recovers surplus food from UCLA dining halls three times a week during the academic year and distributes it to students in need, helping to reduce food waste while addressing food insecurity on campus.

Meal Service Hours:

When: Tuesdays, Wednesdays, and Thursdays at 10:00 PM

Where: Student Activities Center (SAC) Basement, Conference Rooms 1 & 2

Note: Please bring your own containers and utensils to help reduce waste

Important Information:
  • Service runs during weeks 1-10 of each academic quarter
  • Open to all UCLA students, staff, and faculty
  • No sign-up or registration required
  • First-come, first-served basis
  • Follow us for updates on special events and schedule changes
Connect With Us:

@uclabruindine

Emergency Meal Swipes

Emergency meal swipes are available through the Economic Crisis Response Team (ECRT) for students experiencing temporary food insecurity. These swipes can be used at any UCLA dining facility.

Apply for Emergency Swipes Call ECRT

Program Details

The Emergency Meal Swipe program provides immediate food assistance to students experiencing food insecurity. Approved students receive meal swipes that can be used at any UCLA dining facility.

Eligibility & Benefits:
  • Currently enrolled UCLA student
  • FAFSA-eligible students: Up to 7 emergency meal swipes per quarter
  • Non-FAFSA eligible students: Up to 14 emergency meal swipes per quarter
  • No formal application required - contact ECRT directly
How to Apply:
  1. Contact the Economic Crisis Response Team (ECRT) via phone or email
  2. Complete a brief intake with a case manager
  3. If approved, meal swipes will be loaded directly to your BruinCard

ECRT Contact:
(310) 206-0406
ecrt@saonet.ucla.edu

Office Hours: Monday-Friday, 8:30 AM - 4:30 PM

Additional Resources:

Students may also be eligible for other food assistance programs. ECRT can provide information about:

  • CalFresh application assistance
  • On-campus food resources
  • Community food banks and pantries
  • Other emergency financial assistance

Housing Support

UCLA Emergency Housing

Short-term emergency housing is available for students experiencing homelessness or housing insecurity. The program provides temporary accommodations while students work with case managers to secure stable housing.

Apply for Emergency Housing Call for Assistance

Program Details

  • Up to 14 nights of emergency housing per academic year
  • Private or shared accommodations based on availability
  • Case management support to find long-term housing
  • Access to basic amenities (linens, toiletries, etc.)
Contact Information:

Phone: (310) 825-0768 (24/7)

Email: ecrt@saonet.ucla.edu

Location: 106 Bradley Hall, 417 Charles E. Young Drive West

Hours: Monday-Friday, 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM (Walk-ins welcome)

Eligibility:
  • Currently enrolled UCLA student
  • Experiencing homelessness or housing crisis
  • No safe or stable place to stay
  • Willing to work with case managers on a housing plan

USAC Housing Relief Fund

The Undergraduate Students Association Council (USAC) Housing Relief Fund provides financial assistance to UCLA students facing housing insecurity or emergency housing situations.

Apply for Funding Donate to the Fund

Program Details

  • One-time grants up to $500 per academic year
  • Funds can be used for rent, security deposits, or temporary housing
  • Average processing time: 7-10 business days
  • Funds distributed via direct deposit or check
Eligibility Criteria:
  • Currently enrolled UCLA undergraduate student
  • Demonstrated financial need
  • Facing housing insecurity or emergency housing situation
  • Must submit supporting documentation
  • Must meet with a case manager
Contact:
